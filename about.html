Our Story

An elevated escape above the city

Perched on the 18th floor of Escada Apartments with an unobstructed 270-degree sweep of the Nairobi skyline, our rooftop was built for lingering — long lunches that drift into golden hour, first dates that turn into nightcaps, and celebrations that need a view to match. By day it's sun-drenched coffee and single-origin espresso; by night, hand-crafted cocktails, a curated playlist, and city lights stretching to the horizon.

  • Indoor lounge + open-air rooftop seating
  • Full bar with 13 signature cocktails & classics
  • Kitchen serving Shell & Fin, Grill & Thrill, Leafy & Lovely
  • Step-free, wheelchair-accessible access

Know Before You Go

Everything for the perfect visit

🌇

Stunning sunset views

Guests consistently call out the panoramic 270° skyline views — arrive an hour before sunset to catch golden hour from the best seats.

📅

Reserve ahead — it fills up fast

The rooftop is one of the busiest in the city on weekends and evenings. Use "Find a Table" to lock in your spot in advance.

🪪

Bring valid identification

A quick security check is standard at the entrance. Keep a government-issued ID handy to clear the door smoothly.

What Makes Us, Us

Four things worth knowing

01

Golden Hour, Engineered

Seating is oriented around the skyline so every table gets a slice of the sunset — not just the ones by the rail.

02

A Bar Program With a Point of View

Thirteen house signatures — from a vegan whiskey sour to a smoked bourbon fashioned — built in-house, not off a syrup bottle.

03

Shell & Fin, Grill & Thrill and Leafy & Lovely pull from the coast, the grill and the garden — there's a seat at the table for everyone.
