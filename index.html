Our Story

An elevated escape above the city

Perched high above Nairobi with an unobstructed 270-degree sweep of the skyline, our rooftop was built for lingering — long lunches that drift into golden hour, first dates that turn into nightcaps, and celebrations that need a view to match. By day it's sun-drenched brunch and single-origin coffee; by night, hand-crafted cocktails, a curated playlist, and the city lights stretching to the horizon.

  • Indoor lounge + open-air rooftop seating
  • Full bar with signature & classic cocktails
  • Kitchen serving brunch through late night
  • Step-free, wheelchair-accessible access
