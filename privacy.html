Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

270° Rooftop ("we", "us", "our") runs this website as a simple, static informational and reservation-request site. This page explains what data the site touches, in plain language.

What's stored in your browser

This site doesn't have a server-side database or account system. Where it needs to remember something between visits, it uses your browser's own localStorage — data that stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else automatically. Specifically:

  • Day/Night theme choice — which color palette you last picked.
  • Your rating — if you use the star-rating widget on the Reviews page.
  • Draft reservation details — if you submit the reservation form, your name, phone, date, time and party size are saved locally so the "upcoming reservation" banner can show it back to you. This currently only saves to your own browser — it is not yet emailed or sent to 270° Rooftop's team, so a phone/WhatsApp confirmation (see Visit page) is the reliable way to guarantee a table until that's wired up.

You can clear any of this at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this page, or by using the "Cancel" button on the reservation banner.

What we don't do

  • No cookies, no third-party analytics, and no ad trackers are loaded by this site.
  • No third-party fonts or scripts are loaded from external CDNs — everything (fonts included) is served from this same site.
  • We don't sell, share, or have access to anything stored in your browser's localStorage — it never leaves your device through this site's own code.

External links

Links to Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Maps and similar services take you to those companies' own sites or apps, which have their own separate privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to 270° Rooftop via 0702 455 665 or WhatsApp.

Note: this is a plain-language summary written for a small hospitality business, not a lawyer-drafted legal document. If 270° Rooftop later adds real analytics, a payment processor, or an email marketing tool, this page should be updated (and ideally reviewed by a lawyer) to reflect that.
